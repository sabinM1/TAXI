----- Arduino Soldering Quick Guide -----

Tools needed: Soldering iron (350-400°C), flux, perfboard, helping hands

1. Prototype on breadboard first — test everything before soldering
2. Heat the JOINT (pad + lead), not the solder strip directly
3. Touch solder to the hot joint — it should flow and form a shiny cone
4. Let cool naturally; don't blow on it
5. Use IC sockets instead of soldering chips directly
6. Hot glue provides strain relief for wire connections
7. Clean flux residue with isopropyl alcohol

================================================================================

Manufacturer: GensTattu
Type: High Voltage Lithium Polymer (LiHV)
Configuration: 3S1P
Quantity: 5 pcs

Specifications:
- Nominal Voltage: 11.4V (3.8V/cell × 3)
- Full Charge (LiHV): 13.05V (4.35V/cell)
- Capacity: 300mAh (minimum)
- Continuous Discharge: 75C → 22.5 Amps
- Max Burst Discharge: 150C → 45 Amps
- Dimensions: ~68mm × 12mm × 6mm
- Weight: ~8g (±5g)

LiHV vs Standard LiPo:
- LiHV charges to 4.35V/cell (vs 4.20V for LiPo)
- Extra voltage = more motor speed and power
- Can be charged as standard LiPo (to 4.20V/cell) for longer lifespan
- Storage voltage: 3.80-3.85V/cell

Charging:
- MUST use LiHV-capable charger (LiHV mode, 4.35V/cell)
- Charge at 0.3-1.5A (1C-5C), balance charge ALWAYS
- Never exceed 4.35V per cell

Run Time Estimate (mini sumo):
- 300mAh / (2 motors × ~1.2A each typical) ≈ 7.5 minutes
- Burst current capability for aggressive pushing

SAFETY:
- Charge in fireproof LiPo bag, store in fireproof container
- Never overcharge, over-discharge, puncture, or short
- Dispose of swollen/damaged batteries properly

Source: GensTattu official product page, general LiHV battery specifications
