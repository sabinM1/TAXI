Step 1: Attack Base Speed (PID_BASE_SPEED_ATTACK)

Robot's forward speed % when attacking. Higher = faster closing but harder to control. Start at 60, increase until wobble appears, then back off 10.

80

💡 Test: Robot should track opponent smoothly without overshooting. If it wobbles left-right, reduce speed.

Step 2: Lock-On Gain (PID_KP_LOCK)

Aggressiveness of initial centering when opponent first detected. Higher = faster snap-to-center. Too high causes oscillation on first contact.

35.0

💡 Test: Start robot at 45° angle to opponent. Time how fast it centers. If it overshoots past center and corrects back, Kp is too high.

Step 3: Tracking Damping (PID_KD_TRACK)

Derivative gain that smooths tracking after lock-on. Higher = less oscillation but slower correction. The "Soft Turn" personality.

15.0

💡 Test: During sustained tracking (2+ seconds), robot should follow without jerky corrections. Increase Kd if it oscillates, decrease if it feels "lazy."

Step 4: Distance-Gated Attack Zones

Define when the robot switches from cautious approach to full attack.
